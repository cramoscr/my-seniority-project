# $10K/month Role Target Analysis

## Purpose

The central question of My Seniority Project is not simply:

> How do I get a USD 10,000/month job?

It is:

> Which technology individual-contributor roles are actually compensated at or above roughly USD 10,000/month in the relevant global/remote market, what does the market require for those roles, and what is the objective distance between those requirements and my current profile?

This document defines the research and gap-analysis framework that will guide the next phase of the project.

## Deliverable 1 — Target Role Map

Build an evidence-backed table of technology roles that can realistically reach or exceed USD 120K/year total cash/base compensation, prioritizing individual-contributor paths rather than people management.

For each role, capture:

- Role/title and common title variants
- Typical compensation range
- Geography and remote availability
- Industry concentration
- Seniority/experience expectations
- Representative employers
- Confidence/quality of compensation evidence

Candidate families to investigate include, without assuming they are the final targets:

- Staff / Principal Software Engineer
- Staff / Principal Oracle or Database Engineer
- Solutions Architect / Enterprise Architect
- Cloud Architect
- Principal Consultant / Technical Consultant
- Platform / Infrastructure Architect
- Technical Lead roles that remain primarily IC
- Specialized banking/core-systems consulting roles

The research must allow the market evidence to add or eliminate roles.

## Deliverable 2 — What the Market Actually Buys

For each viable target role, analyze real job descriptions and hiring evidence to identify:

- Core responsibilities
- Required technical depth
- Architecture/system-design expectations
- Cloud/platform expectations
- Programming-language expectations
- Domain knowledge
- Leadership without people management
- Communication and stakeholder skills
- Ambiguity and decision-making expectations
- Evidence employers expect to see: projects, scale, certifications, publications, open source, etc.

The objective is to distinguish job-title mythology from observable market requirements.

## Deliverable 3 — Profile Match and Gap Matrix

Compare the target-role requirements against the current professional profile.

Use at least these classifications:

- **Strong match** — already demonstrated with credible evidence
- **Match but weakly signaled** — capability exists but CV/GitHub/LinkedIn/interview evidence is insufficient
- **Partial match** — relevant foundation exists but more depth or current practice is required
- **Material gap** — missing capability likely to block hiring
- **Irrelevant gap** — weakness that does not materially affect the target role

This distinction is critical. The project should not treat every missing technology or credential as something that needs to be learned.

## Deliverable 4 — Prioritized Gap-Closing Plan

Every proposed learning activity, certification, project, or career action should answer:

> Which target-role requirement does this close, and how will we prove that it has been closed?

Prioritize actions by:

1. Impact on eligibility for the target compensation band
2. Size of the current gap
3. Time required to close it
4. Ability to create externally visible evidence
5. Reuse across multiple target roles

This explicitly guards against accumulating courses or certifications that create activity without materially increasing market value.

## Deliverable 5 — Probability and Time-Horizon Assessment

After the market research and gap analysis, estimate qualitatively the plausibility of reaching the compensation target across time horizons such as:

- 6 months
- 12 months
- 24 months

The assessment must identify assumptions and distinguish between:

- Costa Rica/local employment
- LATAM remote employment
- US/international remote employment
- Consulting/contracting

The objective is not false numerical precision. It is to identify which route has the strongest combination of compensation ceiling, fit, probability, and acceptable personal cost.

## Decision Principle

The project is now **market-backward rather than course-forward**.

We first determine what organizations pay USD 10K/month for, then determine which of those problems/roles best match the existing profile, then invest effort only in the highest-value gaps.

The intended result is not to transform the candidate into a generic modern engineer. It is to identify the highest-value intersection between:

**existing experience + demonstrated strengths + achievable gaps + market demand + compensation.**
