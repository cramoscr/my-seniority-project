# My Seniority Project

> **Complexity inside. Clarity outside.**

A deliberate personal effort to develop, test, and demonstrate high-impact
senior engineering capabilities.

## Purpose

Seniority is more than technical knowledge, years of experience, or a job title.

This project focuses on developing and demonstrating four capabilities:

- **Engineering Depth** — architecture, enterprise systems, cloud, and relevant technologies.
- **Professional Judgment** — framing problems, evaluating trade-offs, and making sound decisions under uncertainty.
- **Communication** — turning complex reasoning into clear, concise, and defensible communication.
- **Professional Impact** — connecting technology and engineering decisions to meaningful business outcomes.

## Approach

Professional growth should produce evidence, not just knowledge.

**Learn → Apply → Evaluate → Refine → Demonstrate**

Personal reflection may help identify opportunities for improvement, but this
repository documents the **professional insights, actions, and evidence** that
result from that process.

## Project Structure

- [`roadmap/`](roadmap/) — direction, priorities, and progress
- [`development/`](development/) — capabilities currently being developed

The structure will evolve only as the project requires it.

## Objective

Develop and demonstrate the judgment, capability, clarity, and impact expected
from a high-impact senior engineer.
