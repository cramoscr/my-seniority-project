# Seniority Development Roadmap

This roadmap defines the high-level development path for **My Seniority Project**.

The objective is to strengthen and demonstrate the capabilities required to
operate as a high-impact senior engineer.

The roadmap is iterative. New evidence may change priorities, reveal new gaps,
or demonstrate that some capabilities are already sufficiently strong.

---

## [ ] Phase 1 — Assessment & Direction

**Goal:** Understand the current professional baseline and determine where
development effort can produce the greatest impact.

### Checklist

- [ ] Identify existing strengths and evidence of senior-level capability.
- [ ] Identify meaningful technical and professional gaps.
- [ ] Evaluate target roles and market expectations.
- [ ] Distinguish perceived gaps from evidence-based gaps.
- [ ] Prioritize development areas.
- [ ] Define the initial development focus.

### Expected Outcome

- [ ] Establish a clear and evidence-based picture of:

  **Where I am → Where I want to operate → What actually needs to change**

---

## [ ] Phase 2 — Strengthen Core Senior Capabilities

**Goal:** Work deliberately on the highest-impact gaps identified during
assessment.

### Engineering Depth

- [ ] Identify the technical capabilities requiring deeper development.
- [ ] Strengthen relevant architecture and system-design skills.
- [ ] Strengthen relevant cloud and infrastructure knowledge.
- [ ] Strengthen enterprise integration capabilities.
- [ ] Continue developing relevant Oracle expertise.
- [ ] Explore AI-assisted systems where professionally relevant.

### Professional Judgment

- [ ] Improve problem framing.
- [ ] Practice trade-off analysis.
- [ ] Improve decision-making under uncertainty.
- [ ] Separate facts, assumptions, hypotheses, and conclusions.
- [ ] Calibrate confidence according to available evidence.

### Communication

- [ ] Transform complex reasoning into clear conclusions.
- [ ] Improve concise technical communication.
- [ ] Adapt communication to different audiences.
- [ ] Improve stakeholder communication.
- [ ] Improve communication in group and public-speaking situations.

> **Complexity inside. Clarity outside.**

### Business Impact

- [ ] Connect engineering decisions with operational outcomes.
- [ ] Evaluate cost, risk, maintainability, and organizational impact.
- [ ] Improve identification of valuable business problems.
- [ ] Practice translating business problems into technical opportunities.

### Expected Outcome

- [ ] Demonstrate observable improvement in the capabilities that most strongly
      affect professional impact.

---

## [ ] Phase 3 — Apply Through Real Problems

**Goal:** Move development from learning into practice.

### Checklist

- [ ] Solve realistic engineering and business problems.
- [ ] Design architectures and technical proposals.
- [ ] Build prototypes and experiments.
- [ ] Analyze real operational problems.
- [ ] Practice technical and stakeholder communication.
- [ ] Document important decisions and trade-offs.
- [ ] Review results and identify lessons learned.

### Expected Outcome

- [ ] Demonstrate capabilities through actual work rather than self-assessment.

---

## [ ] Phase 4 — Build Evidence

**Goal:** Convert professional growth into externally observable evidence.

### Checklist

- [ ] Document relevant technical projects.
- [ ] Produce architecture case studies.
- [ ] Complete strategically relevant certifications.
- [ ] Maintain evidence through GitHub repositories.
- [ ] Produce professional or technical writing.
- [ ] Document meaningful technical experiments.
- [ ] Document business problems analyzed or solved.
- [ ] Document significant engineering decisions.
- [ ] Capture measurable outcomes whenever possible.

### Expected Outcome

- [ ] Build a body of evidence supporting high-impact senior engineering
      positioning.

---

## [ ] Phase 5 — Market Validation

**Goal:** Test whether the developed profile creates value in the real market.

### Checklist

- [ ] Identify relevant senior and high-impact roles.
- [ ] Compare capabilities against real job requirements.
- [ ] Test professional positioning.
- [ ] Apply selectively to relevant opportunities.
- [ ] Interview.
- [ ] Gather external feedback.
- [ ] Identify recurring market gaps.
- [ ] Adjust the development roadmap based on evidence.

### Expected Outcome

- [ ] Obtain external evidence that the professional profile is competitive for
      the targeted level of responsibility and impact.

---

## Continuous Cycle

The phases are not strictly sequential.

**Assess → Prioritize → Develop → Apply → Demonstrate → Validate → Reassess**

Projects, interviews, market feedback, and professional experiences may send
the process back to an earlier phase.

---

## Current Status

**Current primary phase:** Phase 1 — Assessment & Direction

Some activities from later phases may run in parallel when useful.

> **Depth and execution matter more than a long list of goals.**
