## 2026-09-01 — Cognitive Assessment v1.0 completed

### Milestone

Completed the first structured assessment track across three sessions covering:

- Logical, abstract, and quantitative reasoning
- Verbal reasoning and working memory
- Rule acquisition and formal inference
- Applied problem solving
- Decision-making under ambiguity
- Senior-level engineering judgment

Detailed scores and diagnostic interpretation are maintained in the private seniority-development repository.

### High-Level Findings

The assessment did **not** indicate broad cognitive capacity as a primary barrier to higher-impact technical roles.

Strong professional signals included:

- Systems reasoning
- Abstract and applied problem solving
- Working memory
- Technical diagnosis
- Risk reasoning
- Business/technical trade-off judgment
- Senior-level engineering judgment

Development areas identified for deliberate practice:

- Formal logic precision: necessary/sufficient conditions, converse errors, and contraposition
- Numerical precision in percentage-change problems
- Decision-making with incomplete-but-sufficient evidence
- Avoiding unnecessary certainty-seeking before making a defensible decision

### Directional Change

The working hypothesis is shifting away from **accumulating broad additional knowledge as the default development strategy**.

Greater emphasis should be placed on:

- Demonstrating existing senior capability
- Communicating complex reasoning clearly
- Producing evidence of professional impact
- Strengthening targeted gaps rather than studying indiscriminately
- Testing professional positioning against the external market
